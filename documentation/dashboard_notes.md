# Dashboard metric definitions

- **Net Sales** = Gross Sales × (1 − Discount Rate)
- **Profit** = Net Sales − Cost
- **Margin %** = Profit ÷ Net Sales
- **AOV** = Net Sales ÷ order count

## Recommended review flow
1. Check headline KPIs.
2. Review monthly sales/profit trend.
3. Compare regions.
4. Compare channels.
5. Investigate low-margin categories.

## Workbook structure
- **Dashboard** — KPI cards and management charts
- **Raw_Data** — transaction-level source table
- **Monthly_Summary** — monthly revenue/profit/order view
- **Region_Summary** — regional KPIs
- **Channel_Summary** — channel KPIs
- **Category_Summary** — category revenue, profit and margin
